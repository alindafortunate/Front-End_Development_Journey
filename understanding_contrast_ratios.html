🎨 Understanding WCAG Contrast Ratios

Why contrast matters for making your website accessible to everyone

1️⃣ Normal Text (under 18px) — Minimum 4.5:1

What it means: For regular-sized text (like body text), the contrast ratio must be at least 4.5:1 to be readable.

2️⃣ Large Text (18px+ or bold 14px+) — Minimum 3:1

What it means: For large text (like headings), the contrast ratio only needs to be 3:1 because the larger size makes it easier to read.

🔍 How to Check Contrast Ratios

  • Use browser DevTools: Chrome/Firefox DevTools show contrast ratios in the Styles panel
  • Online tools: WebAIM Contrast Checker or Contrast Checker
  • Browser extensions: "Wave" or "Axe" accessibility tools
  • Formula: Contrast ratio = (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), where L1 and L2 are relative luminance values

💡 Quick Reference Card

✅ Good Contrast
  • #000 on #FFF (21:1)
  • #333 on #FFF (8.6:1)
  • #FFF on #1a365d (12.3:1)
❌ Poor Contrast
  • #999 on #FFF (2.1:1)
  • #CCC on #FFF (1.5:1)
  • #666 on #888 (1.1:1)
🎯 Golden Rule: If in doubt, choose higher contrast. Your users will thank you!
